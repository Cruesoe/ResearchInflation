[b]Every research project you finish makes the rest of the tree more expensive.[/b]

Made for long, multi-generational colonies: early techs stay cheap, but by the late eras each project needs a dedicated, well-equipped research team.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Each finished project raises the cost of every remaining project by a set percentage, compounding: cost × (1 + rate) ^ finished projects.
[*]Points you have already earned are kept. Only the remaining work costs more, and finished research stays finished.
[*]The research tab shows the real, inflated cost, and that is the cost a project finishes at.
[*]Techprints for an unlocked project still give half the remaining (inflated) cost.
[*]The settings page shows what the whole tree will cost from scratch, by era, and your colony's current multiplier.
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Inflation per finished project[/b]: how much each finish raises remaining costs, from 0% to 10%. Default 1%.
[*][b]Maximum multiplier[/b]: stops costs rising past a multiple of their starting cost. Default no cap.
[*][b]Ignore projects cheaper than[/b]: cheap projects below this vanilla cost are not inflated and do not raise the multiplier. Default 0.
[*][b]Era costs[/b]: leave vanilla costs alone, add a fixed amount per era, or replace each era's costs with your own. Default vanilla.
[/list]
Settings changes apply straight away, including to a game in progress.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Works with modded research trees, including Vanilla Factions Expanded - Tribals and the Progression mods. Anomaly knowledge projects are left alone.
Safe to add to an existing save. In-progress research keeps its percentage when the mod is added.
