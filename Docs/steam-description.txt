[h2]Research Inflation — Major Update[/h2]
Research now actually costs what the research tab shows. Progress can no longer run past the total.

[h3]Fixes[/h3]
[list]
[*] Inflated cost is the real completion cost, not a display trick. The bar, the tooltip, and finishing a project all use the same number.
[*] Tech-level penalties are kept, so researching above your era no longer makes progress exceed the listed cost.
[*] Finished projects stay finished when inflation changes.
[*] Costs update as soon as a tech completes, instead of lagging for a second or two.
[/list]

[h3]How inflation works now[/h3]
[list]
[*] Points you have already earned are kept. Only the remaining work gets more expensive.
[*] Existing saves are converted once so in-progress projects keep the percent you already had.
[/list]

[h3]Settings[/h3]
[list]
[*] Live colony dashboard: remaining tree cost, work left, current project, and what the next finish does to the rest of the tree.
[*] Breakdown by era, plus loaded-modlist projections if no colony is open.
[*] Optional maximum multiplier so huge modded trees do not explode late game (0 = no cap).
[*] Era extras are still there: add to vanilla cost or replace it, per tech level.
[/list]

Anomaly knowledge projects are still left alone. Harmony is still required.


[h1]Research Inflation[/h1]
Are you tired of your colonists mastering spaceflight in just three years? Do you want a playthrough that feels like a true, multi-generational epic where every technological leap is a hard-fought victory?

[b]Research Inflation[/b] dynamically overhauls RimWorld’s pacing by pitting your colony's scientific output against the relentless force of compounding inflation. 

[h2]How It Works[/h2]
Instead of static price tags, this mod actively tracks your technological progress. [b]Every time you finish a research project, the point cost of every remaining technology in the game increases.[/b]

[list]
[*][b]The Math:[/b] The game uses a compounding formula: [i]Base Era Cost * (1.01 ^ Completed Projects)[/i].
[*][b]The Result:[/b] Your first Neolithic tech will only take a few days for a single researcher. By the time you reach the Ultra era, technologies will cost thousands of points, requiring a dedicated, highly skilled, and fully equipped laboratory team to complete.
[/list]

[h2](In-Game Settings[/h2]
You are entirely in control of your saga's pacing. Using the Mod Settings menu, you can adjust everything on the fly—no game restart required:
[list]
[*][b]Inflation Rate:[/b] Adjust the per-tech compound penalty anywhere from 1.0% to 5.0% (Defaults to 1.0%).
[*][b]Era Baselines:[/b] Set distinct starting costs for Neolithic, Medieval, Industrial, Spacer, and Ultra technologies. (Or disable this feature entirely.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility & Performance[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]100% Save Safe:[/b] This mod does [i]not[/i] inject custom data into your save file. You can safely add or remove it at any point in your playthrough without breaking your game. 
[/list]
